# [recap 25/09/02] Il drago guardiano - L'attacco degli Zoroin

*by **Zoddo** on 2002-10-05 15:29 UTC*

But my Father said

„Cursed are you, Caine,

who killed your brother.

As I was cast out

so shall you be.”

e bada arleqine tu farai la stessa fine di Crone

Caine said. "Thank you, Mother," and

With that, moving in quick movements,

Caine took the stake of gopher wood,

seized it and drove it

deep within the Crone's heart.

teclis io gioco nell'unico gruppo di gaeta che pero' in this moment è in pausa
